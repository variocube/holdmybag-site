## Getting there and access

The lockers 1-20 and the control unit is located in mezzanine level of the hotel. Lockers 21-30 are on the ground floor. Just 6 minutes’ walk from Monastiraki Metro Station (400m). The hotel entrance is through the passage of Noel cocktail bar. If the door is closed (maybe between 02:00 and 07:00) please ask the security personnel to open it for you!

## Opening hours

Open 24/7.

## Booking and payment

- Online: Reserve and pay for a locker in advance; the access code arrives by email. [Book now](https://booking.holdmybag.net/zeusrfid/athens-commercial/dates)
- On site by smartphone: Scan the QR code on the locker, choose a compartment and pay on your phone. Payment methods: Card, Apple Pay, Google Pay, PayPal.
- On site at the terminal: not available at this location.
