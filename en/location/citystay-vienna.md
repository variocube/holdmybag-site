## Getting there and access

City Stay Vienna Englisch: Dear guest! We are pleased to introduce you to our new luggage storage service! Our lockers meet strict security standards and have a modern user interface. To get there, open the black metal door next to the drinks machine on the ground floor, go down the stairs, turn left and continue straight on until you reach the laundry room. ## Booking and payment

- Online: Reserve and pay for a locker in advance; the access code arrives by email. [Book now](https://booking.holdmybag.net/elenas-apartments/city-stay-vienna-stephansplatz/dates)
- On site by smartphone: Scan the QR code on the locker, choose a compartment and pay on your phone.
- On site at the terminal: Choose a compartment on the screen and pay by card or contactless at the terminal. Payment methods: Card, Apple Pay, Google Pay.
