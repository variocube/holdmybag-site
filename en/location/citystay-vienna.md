## Getting there and access

Dear Guest! We are pleased to introduce our new luggage storage service! Our lockers meet strict security standards and feature a modern user interface. You can reserve your preferred storage time either in advance or upon arrival. The lockers are located in the basement of the building, directly next to the laundry room. To reach them, open the black metal door next to the vending machine on the ground floor, go down the stairs, turn left, and continue straight ahead until you reach the laundry room. After collecting your luggage, you can easily take the elevator back to the ground floor. The premises are secured with security code 24/7. We are happy to assist you with any questions or concerns and can be reached at any time by phone at +43, 660, or 6614006. Thank you for your trust! ## Booking and payment

- Online: Reserve and pay for a locker in advance; the access code arrives by email. [Book now](https://booking.holdmybag.net/elenas-apartments/city-stay-vienna-stephansplatz/dates)
- On site by smartphone: Scan the QR code on the locker, choose a compartment and pay on your phone.
- On site at the terminal: Choose a compartment on the screen and pay by card or contactless at the terminal. Payment methods: Card, Apple Pay, Google Pay.
